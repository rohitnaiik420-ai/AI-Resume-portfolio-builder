AI TIPWriting a Great Summary

Mention: your role + years of experience, top 2-3 skills, and what value you bring. Keep it under 3 sentences.

AI TIPEducation Section

List most recent first. Include relevant coursework, honors, GPA (above 3.5), and extracurriculars.

AI TIPWork Experience

Use action verbs (Built, Led, Increased). Include quantifiable results. Use bullet points (one per line starting with •).

AI TIPSkills That Get Noticed

Include technical skills (React, Python, SQL) and soft skills (Leadership, Communication). Aim for 8-15 skills.

AI TIPShowcasing Projects

Include personal, academic, or open source projects. Mention technologies used, your role, and impact or results.
